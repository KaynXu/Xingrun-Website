# Starain Brand And Homepage Design

## Overview

This spec defines the approved brand and homepage direction for the current Xingrun landing experience.

The goal is to reposition the public-facing site from a generic dark AI SaaS aesthetic to a brighter, more trustworthy AI education product identity built around the new brand name `Starain`.

This direction is based on the following confirmed decisions:

- Brand name should shift from `星润 AI 教育解决方案` to `Starain`
- The homepage should feel like a bright, future-facing AI education product rather than a black-background AI tool site
- The site should speak primarily to school and institution decision makers, while still feeling credible to teachers, parents, and students
- The strongest positioning is not "we are just an AI Edu platform" but "we productized workflows proven inside our own education organization"
- The preferred visual direction is balanced bright futurism: advanced and clean, but still trustworthy and education-oriented

## Product Positioning

### Core identity

`Starain` is an `AI Edu Platform`.

However, the homepage should not rely on category alone. The defining story is that Starain grew out of a real education organization solving its own operational and teaching workflow problems first, then turning those validated workflows into a SaaS product for peer institutions.

### Core narrative

The homepage should communicate this sequence:

1. We are from a real teaching environment
2. We solved these workflow problems for ourselves first
3. We turned the proven workflow into a reusable SaaS product
4. Other education organizations can now use the same system to upgrade their own processes

### Recommended message hierarchy

- Brand: `Starain`
- Category: `AI Edu Platform`
- Value statement: `教育工作流终于被 AI 重新组织好了`
- Supporting story: Starain comes from real teaching practice and productizes workflows already validated inside an education organization

## Audience

### Primary audience

School and education institution decision makers.

### Secondary audiences

- Teachers
- Teaching operations and research teams
- Parents and students who may also visit the site

### Audience implication

The design must lead with trust and clarity before showing technical sophistication. Users should first think "this is a serious education product" and only then feel "this is powered by advanced AI workflows."

## Chosen Visual Direction

### Direction name

Balanced Bright Futurism.

### What this means

The site should be visually bright, structured, and calm, with a limited amount of intelligent, future-facing detail. It should not look like a dark cyber AI product, but it should also avoid looking like a generic school website or a soft tutoring landing page.

### Visual priorities

- Bright and breathable
- Professional and trustworthy
- Forward-looking and productized
- Structured and operational
- Education-first, not hype-first

### Visual directions to avoid

- Pure black AI dashboard aesthetics
- Heavy blue-purple glow or cyberpunk language
- Child-focused tutoring visuals
- Traditional school portal visuals
- Generic enterprise SaaS minimalism with no education character

## Brand Expression

### Brand architecture

Use the following brand hierarchy on the homepage:

- Primary brand: `Starain`
- Secondary descriptor: `AI Edu Platform`

Do not keep `星润 AI 教育解决方案` as the dominant public-facing brand line in the hero.

### Brand tone

Starain should feel like:

- A real product brand
- Built from practice
- Quietly advanced
- Trusted by educators

It should not feel like:

- A temporary project page
- A generic "solution" company page
- A pure software startup disconnected from education

## Homepage Messaging

### Approved hero direction

Recommended hero copy:

- Brand: `Starain`
- Descriptor: `AI Edu Platform`
- Main headline: `教育工作流终于被 AI 重新组织好了`
- Supporting copy: `Starain 起源于真实教学场景。我们先为自己的机构解决复习资料、题库沉淀、讲义生成与教学协同的问题，再把这套已验证的工作流产品化，帮助更多教育团队完成 AI 化升级。`
- Primary CTA: `查看平台方案`
- Secondary CTA: `申请试用`

### Messaging principles

- Lead with outcome, not category jargon
- Make the origin story concrete
- Emphasize validated workflow instead of abstract AI claims
- Speak like an operator who built tools from real pain points

## Homepage Structure

The current page structure is fundamentally usable and should be retained, but each section should be reframed around workflow proof rather than feature listing alone.

### Section 1: Hero

Purpose:

- Establish Starain as the new product brand
- Show the platform category
- Deliver the core narrative and value statement
- Introduce the productized-from-practice story

Visual behavior:

- Bright cold-white background
- Light blue/cyan atmospheric gradients
- Minimal knowledge-network or flow-line motifs
- No dominant dark video background

### Section 2: Core capabilities as workflow stages

Purpose:

- Show not just what features exist, but how a real teaching workflow has been organized

Recommended framing:

- `教学素材接入`
- `AI 结构化处理`
- `教学交付输出`
- `团队沉淀与复用`

Existing feature modules can map into these workflow stages:

- Review generation
- Mistake tracking
- Question-bank accumulation and paper generation
- Handout and lesson material generation

### Section 3: Workflow process

Purpose:

- Demonstrate that Starain is a coherent process, not a pile of disconnected features

Recommended three-step model:

1. `教学素材进入平台`
2. `AI 完成结构化处理`
3. `输出到复习与教学协同`

Each step should reference real teaching artifacts such as notes, recordings, questions, review packets, handouts, and reusable knowledge assets.

### Section 4: About Starain

Purpose:

- Explain why Starain has the right to exist
- Reinforce the product's origin from actual educational operations

This section should answer:

- Who are we?
- Why did we build this?
- Why should another institution trust this product?

The answer should emphasize that the product emerged from internal operational need and real usage, not from a top-down pitch deck.

## Visual System

### Color palette

Recommended core colors:

- Page background: `#F6FBFF`
- Secondary surface: `#EEF6FF`
- Headline text: `#16324F`
- Body text: `#334155`
- Muted text: `#64748B`
- Primary brand blue: `#2F80ED`
- Highlight cyan: `#22C7E8`
- Support teal: `#14B8A6`

### Color rules

- Backgrounds should remain predominantly light
- Dark colors should be used for typography and selective contrast only
- Purple should not be a leading accent anymore
- Gradients should be subtle and cool-toned, not neon

### Background treatment

Preferred page atmosphere:

- Mostly bright cold-white canvas
- Very soft blue/cyan fog or radial gradients
- Optional subtle flow-line or node patterns
- Motion, if used, should feel calm and intelligent rather than flashy

### Card style

Cards should shift from dark glass panels to light product surfaces:

- White or near-white cards
- Light blue outlines or neutral cool borders
- Soft shadows
- Generous rounding
- Occasional glassmorphism only in moderation

Target feeling:

- Premium education technology product
- Organized and readable
- Quietly advanced

## Component Guidance

### Hero layout

Recommended structure:

- Left: brand, descriptor, headline, supporting copy, CTAs
- Right: a composed product visualization of a teaching workflow dashboard

The right-side visual should show practical system outputs rather than generic AI decoration. Example elements:

- Review packet card
- Question bank module
- Handout draft card
- Knowledge node map
- Team collaboration status

### Buttons

Use a clear two-level button system:

- Primary button: filled blue or blue-cyan, white text
- Secondary button: white or translucent light surface, cool border, dark text

Avoid heavy dark buttons on the bright homepage.

### Icons and diagrams

Use icons to support comprehension, not as the main decorative device.

Prefer:

- Workflow arrows
- Knowledge nodes
- Small structured status blocks
- Teaching artifact cards

Avoid:

- Overly abstract 3D tech graphics
- Strong neon glows
- Dense dashboard clutter

## Copy Guidance For Existing Feature Blocks

The current landing page already references the right product areas, but the language should shift from feature list to workflow proof.

### Replace isolated feature framing with workflow framing

Recommended transitions:

- `课后复习系统` -> `从课堂素材到复习交付`
- `智能错题本` -> `把错误沉淀成可追踪资产`
- `国际课程题库 / 自动组卷` -> `把题目沉淀成可调用的题库系统`
- `教案与讲义生成` -> `把课程目标转化为讲义与教研交付`

### Copy style

- Operational, not fluffy
- Specific, not vague
- Productized, not service-only
- Grounded in artifacts and workflow outputs

## Non-Goals

This redesign should not attempt to become:

- A parent-facing consumer tutoring site
- A school administration ERP site
- A purely technical AI lab page
- A heavily animated launch microsite

## Rollout Priority

When implementing the visual refresh, prioritize in this order:

1. Rebrand the landing hero from `星润 AI 教育解决方案` to `Starain`
2. Replace the dark hero visual system with the approved bright futuristic system
3. Update headline, supporting copy, and CTA hierarchy
4. Reframe feature cards into workflow-stage cards
5. Refresh process and about sections to reinforce the origin story
6. Extend the light visual system to legal pages and authenticated surfaces later as a second pass

## Success Criteria

The redesign is successful if a first-time visitor can quickly infer all of the following:

- Starain is a real AI education product, not just a concept page
- The product came from real teaching operations
- The platform is trustworthy enough for institutions
- The AI aspect feels advanced but not theatrical
- The homepage tells one coherent story from inputs to outputs to reusable institutional value
